# Azul website product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Static-first Astro website.

## Audience

Small businesses starting out or improving an ineffective online presence. Azul is based in Orlando, serves Central Florida, and works with remote clients across the United States.

## Visitor purpose

Help a visitor understand Azul's services, typical prices, working process, and contact options.

## Pages

- `/`: introduction, services overview, process, and founder.
- `/services`: packages, individual services, pricing guidance, FAQs, and enquiry.
- `/how-we-work`: the steps from first call to launch.
- `/about`: Saymon's public founder story and working approach.
- `/privacy`: public enquiry-data information.

Navigation includes Home, Services, How We Work, and About. Privacy is linked from the footer.

## Public content

Use [the public content contract](../../docs/website/Public%20Content%20Contract.md) for approved facts and claims. The site code remains the reference for exact shipped wording and behavior.

## Experience

- Keep core copy, prices, navigation, email contact, and the ordinary form usable without JavaScript.
- Use semantic structure, responsive layouts, keyboard support, visible focus, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Do not imply conceptual images are real client work or invent outcomes, testimonials, or credentials.
- Keep personal and company-private notes out of this repository.
